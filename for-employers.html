For employers

Help workers turn real consistency into portable evidence.

You Paid is building an employer and workforce-data contribution layer so qualifying organizations can help verify the work history their people have already earned.

1

Authorize the relationship

Define the organization, permitted data, consumer authorization or other lawful basis, verification scope, and operational responsibilities.

2

Contribute supported data

Provide only defined employment, payroll, timekeeping, assignment, or related work-history information through an approved channel.

3

Handle corrections

Respond to material accuracy questions and correction requests rather than treating contributed data as permanently unquestionable.

What You Paid wants from a data provider

Authoritative facts, not opinions about a person.

Our strongest early dataset is objective: relationship dates, qualifying work activity, pay periods, clock-ins, workdays, assignments, and other supported facts. We do not need employers to make broad character judgments or provide subjective employee rankings.

Why employers may care

A better record can create value on both sides.

Worker benefit

Employees can preserve evidence of consistency instead of letting years of work disappear into disconnected payroll systems.

Administrative clarity

A defined data contribution process can reduce ad hoc verification requests and create a standardized correction path.

Portable recognition

Workers can carry verified history forward without requiring the employer to make a lending or credit decision.

Partner categories

Not only traditional employers.

Potential contributors include payroll providers, timekeeping platforms, staffing firms, gig platforms, workforce systems, and other organizations that maintain reliable work-activity records.
