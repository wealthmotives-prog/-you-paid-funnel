Privacy & Data Requests

You Paid provides a clear path for people to raise access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other privacy-related requests.

Send requests to:

privacy@joinyoupaid.com

Please include the email address associated with your You Paid account or waitlist entry and state the type of request you are making. Do not email passwords, full Social Security numbers, or unnecessary identity documents.

Types of requests

  • Access to eligible personal information.
  • Correction of inaccurate account or profile information.
  • Deletion of eligible information.
  • Questions about data sources, sharing, or retention.
  • Withdrawal of eligible marketing consent.
  • Concerns about a work-verification record. Verification disputes may also be submitted through the member portal where available.

Identity verification

Before fulfilling a sensitive request, You Paid may need to verify that the requester is the person associated with the data or is otherwise authorized to act. The amount of verification should be proportionate to the sensitivity of the request.

Records we may need to retain

Deletion is not always the same as erasing every trace of an event. Limited records may need to be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, accounting, dispute history, auditability, or to document that a correction or deletion occurred.

Work-record disputes

If your concern is about the accuracy or completeness of a verification record, use the member portal when available or explain the disputed item in your message. You Paid's goal is to preserve the evidence, investigate the source, document the outcome, and correct the record when appropriate.

Timing

Response timing can depend on the nature of the request and applicable law. You Paid will not represent a request as completed until the required review and action have actually occurred.
