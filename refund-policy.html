Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Payments are not live yet.

You Paid does not currently represent secure payment processing as active. This policy establishes the principles that must be shown to a consumer before a paid checkout is activated.

Founding Access

Founding Access is intended to be a one-time purchase at the selected level. Before payment becomes live, checkout will display the exact amount due, the applicable Founding Access Terms, and any refund or cancellation conditions that apply to that transaction.

Duplicate or erroneous charges

If a person is charged more than once for the same intended transaction, charged an amount different from the amount displayed at checkout, or charged after a confirmed cancellation where applicable, the transaction should be reviewed promptly and corrected when the records support the request.

Refund effects

If a Founding Access payment is fully refunded or reversed, the associated paid entitlement may be removed or reduced to the highest level still supported by remaining qualifying payments. A refund does not alter the truth of any separate work-verification record.

Future subscriptions

If You Paid launches a recurring membership, the checkout flow must disclose the billing amount, cadence, renewal terms, cancellation method, and any refund limitations before enrollment. Those subscription-specific terms will control that service.

No hidden fees

You Paid intends to disclose the total amount due before final confirmation. Any taxes, processor charges, setup charges, recurring fees, or other required amounts will be shown where applicable rather than introduced after purchase.

How to request review

For payment or refund questions, contact support@joinyoupaid.com. Legal questions may be sent to legal@joinyoupaid.com.
