What these payments are

Founding access, not an investment.

The $1, $10, and $100 levels are one-time payments for Founding status and the associated founder privileges described for each level. They are separate from any future recurring subscription for work-data verification.

Founding access does not represent equity, ownership, a security, a charitable donation, a loan, a deposit account, a credit account, or a promise that You Paid will furnish information to any consumer reporting agency. Future perks may evolve as the product develops, but the founding designation and the level actually purchased will remain part of the member record subject to the Founding Access Terms.
