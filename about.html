Who we are

We are building recognition around real consistency.

You Paid is a prelaunch product operated by 850 Lifestyle LLC. We are building for people who already show responsibility through work and want a clearer way to preserve that history over time.

See what we're buildingContact us
Why this exists

Too much real effort disappears into the background.

People can show up for months or years, maintain employment, work multiple jobs, or consistently complete contract work without that history being organized into a durable record they can carry forward. You Paid starts from the belief that verified consistency deserves to be preserved accurately.

Our working principle

Recognition before remediation.

We are not starting with the assumption that a person is broken and needs to be fixed. We are starting with evidence of what they already do well.

How we think

Build the record before selling the outcome.

Truth

Historical employment facts should remain separate from the date a member actually joins You Paid.

Evidence

A paid tier, founder badge, or subscription should never make an unsupported work record look verified.

Utility

Our long-term goal is to make verified work history genuinely useful, but we will not claim downstream utility before it exists.

Accountability

Newcomers should know who is behind the product.
