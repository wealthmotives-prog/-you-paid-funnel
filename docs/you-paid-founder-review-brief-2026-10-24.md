**October 24, 2026 | Decision-quality review | Prelaunch**

## Investment thesis to validate
A work-consistency and verification product may be valuable when it differentiates **self-declared history**, **employer-confirmed facts**, and **operator-reviewed decisions**. This thesis is unproven commercially. The product does not presently furnish credit data or promise score improvements.

## Highest-leverage commercial insight
Do not lead with a future credit-score claim. Test the customer proposition: *a transparent, permission-based record of work consistency whose verified and unverified components can be distinguished*. Conduct interviews, test landing-page comprehension and willingness to pay, and establish acceptable employer participation burden. No statistical conclusion is justified from preorders alone.

## Risk prioritization
**P0 (no pilot until resolved):** reliable authenticated employer response intake; trusted employer contact validation, revocation, single-use/concurrency test, rate limiting/WAF; clear consent and privacy notices; operator review path; incident/monitoring; legal/privacy review of representations and handling.
**P1 (limited pilot quality):** mobile/desktop signed-in E2E, accessibility, dispute/appeal paths, correspondence and paper response operational procedures, reconciliation of existing payment and refund workflows.
**P2 (post-pilot):** safe isolated malware evidence scanning and production data exchange to partners, if needed and legally supported.

## Suggested launch sequence
1. **Internal demo**: controlled test users only; review all portal states and retention/security controls.
2. **Synthetic employer QA**: separate authorized test environment, fixture cleanup and independent concurrent HTTP verification.
3. **Small permissioned pilot**: only after P0 approvals, with no CRA or credit-score representations.
4. **Measured validation**: track activation, historical declaration completion, legitimate employer confirmation completion, response times, review load, disputes, refunds and retention.
5. **Commercial decision**: expand, change positioning or stop based on observed outcomes and compliance reality.

## Founder decision gates
- Choose target first customer and the problem solved *without* dependence on a credit bureau.
- Decide what level of employer confirmation is useful, attainable, and affordable.
- Confirm data rights/retention/dispute operations and review claims with competent legal/compliance advisers before a real pilot.
- Set numeric success thresholds before evaluating pilot results (not after).
