# You Paid — Earned Performance Credit: Feasibility Gate 01
Date: October 24, 2026. Draft conceptual research; NOT a credit agreement, not a reported or reportable account, not legal advice.

## Invention hypothesis
A consumer may voluntarily enter a genuine agreement in which documented, separately accepted noncash services/deliverables satisfy a legitimate obligation owed to the provider. The critical questions are whether the provider actually receives value, whether an independent creditor/borrower relationship exists, how terms and performance are serviced, and whether a bureau accepts this product under an appropriate reporting category.

## Bright-line accounting distinction
Employment at an unrelated employer does not transfer value to You Paid automatically. A worker's hours or wages belong to that worker and their contractual employer absent a lawful authorized assignment or a separate genuine exchange. Assigning a score to work effort is not monetary performance and cannot be coded as a payment by merely renaming it.

### Competing structures to investigate (none approved)
A. **Employment evidence subscription**: consumer pays a service fee and organizes verified work; no credit obligation or corresponding repayment history. Suitable for profile/recordkeeping, not a credit tradeline.
B. **Actual credit product with cash repayment**: consumer has bona fide extension of credit, underlying real goods/services/advance, documented due dates and actual payments. Easier to explain than noncash but imposes lending and consumer-credit requirements and reporting duties. Partner with a licensed issuer if necessary.
C. **Genuine noncash satisfaction**: a lawful real obligation exists, You Paid independently benefits from specifically described deliverables, both parties agree to reasonable consideration and valuation, a documented acceptance decision occurs, and servicing records payment/satisfaction truthfully. Counsel must scrutinize employment, tax, consumer credit, labor, unfair/deceptive practices, valuation, and bureau classification before live deployment. Cannot promise positive-only monthly status.
D. **Consumer-controlled noncredit work evidence**: verify and transmit employment evidence to permissible recipients; valuable even if no bureau furnisher agreement.

## Falsification tests (stop if any fail)
1. Does a genuine creditor-consumer obligation arise, rather than a nominal account created primarily for a tradeline?
2. What actual value does You Paid provide, and what does You Paid receive in return?
3. If work is done for an unrelated employer, what legal mechanism gives You Paid a beneficial interest or accepted performance?
4. Does the consumer owe payment under enforceable terms? What objectively constitutes timely/late performance?
5. What happens to unemployed, disabled, leave-taking, disputed, or terminated members? Is unfavorable history truthfully handled?
6. Can an independent CPA reconcile balances, consideration, performance and account aging to bank/general-ledger entries?
7. What Metro 2 classification is factually correct? Has a bureau/authorized receiving intermediary confirmed acceptance and test-file expectations?
8. Could a consumer misunderstand the instrument as already furnishing or guaranteed to improve credit? Correct all such misimpressions before marketing.

## Minimal prototype model (simulation only)
Separate work events (self-declared -> source-confirmed -> operator-reviewed), contractual obligation ledger (actual value and signed terms), satisfaction ledger (genuine accepted cash/noncash transaction evidence, valuation and effective date), internal score (not reported as payment), and furnishing readiness (BLOCKED until independent approval). Build fixture tests for missed deadlines, corrections, disputes, withdrawal, leave and reversals. Never fabricate paid dates or automatically map work grade to Metro 2 status.

## Approval gates
- Written opinion by consumer-credit + employment/tax counsel on account structure and noncash consideration.
- Financial model from CPA/bookkeeping and verifiable benefit to both parties.
- Bureau or approved aggregator pre-application product classification discussion.
- Operational FCRA/Reg V dispute/accuracy and full-file reporting policies.
- Signed permissions, privacy disclosures, servicing and audit trails.
- Only then consider real member credit contract, issuer relationship, and furnished records.
