# You Paid Community Asset Exploration v0.1

Status: Internal strategy note — not a launch plan and not public marketing copy.

## Why this exists

You Paid is exploring whether an on-chain community asset could eventually support participation, rewards, access, or community coordination. The product decision must come before the token.

## Three very different instruments

### 1. Fiat-backed stablecoin

A true stablecoin is intended to maintain a fixed fiat value and typically requires reserve, redemption, compliance, custody, sanctions/AML, and operating controls appropriate to the jurisdictions involved. On Solana, the Token-2022 stack supports stablecoin-specific controls such as pausability and permanent delegate functionality.

A You Paid stablecoin should not be launched casually or marketed as a speculative community coin.

### 2. Community / utility / loyalty token

A non-stable community asset could potentially represent rewards, access, reputation-linked benefits, or ecosystem participation. Its legal and product treatment depends on how it is sold, distributed, marketed, transferred, and whether holders are led to expect profits from You Paid's efforts.

This path requires separate legal review before public launch or sale.

### 3. Speculative launchpad coin

Pump.fun is designed around rapid coin creation and trading. That is structurally different from issuing a fiat-backed stablecoin with reserve and redemption obligations.

Using a speculative launchpad for a product marketed around financial recognition could create unnecessary brand, consumer-protection, and regulatory risk.

## Current recommendation

1. Keep all token concepts out of the primary consumer funnel for now.
2. Define the actual community problem the asset would solve.
3. Decide whether the asset should be stable, non-transferable, closed-loop, utility/rewards, or freely transferable.
4. If the goal is a stablecoin, use a regulated issuance/custody/compliance path rather than a memecoin launchpad.
5. If the goal is community rewards, first test a non-transferable or closed-loop points/credential system.
6. Do not connect Founding Access payments to promises of token appreciation, future profits, yield, or resale value.
7. Do not imply that buying Founding Access guarantees a future token allocation unless a compliant program is separately designed and disclosed.
8. Require legal, tax, sanctions/AML, custody, and consumer-protection review before public token issuance.

## Product sequence

Founding Access → member identity → verified work history → closed-loop founder/community benefits → optional on-chain credential/reward experiments → transferable token only if a real use case and compliant structure justify it.

## Decision questions

- What problem does the asset solve better than ordinary database points?
- Why must it be transferable?
- Why must it be stable?
- Who can mint, burn, pause, freeze, or redeem it?
- What backs it?
- What does a holder have a legal right to receive?
- What happens if the issuer fails?
- What jurisdictions will be supported?
- How are sanctions, fraud, and mistaken transfers handled?
- How will consumers understand the difference between You Paid services and the token?
