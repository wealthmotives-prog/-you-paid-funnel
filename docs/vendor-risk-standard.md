# You Paid Vendor Risk Standard v0.1

Status: Draft operating standard

Every vendor that can access You Paid systems, personal information, verification evidence, payment information, support records, or reporting data must be inventoried before production use.

## Minimum review fields

- Vendor name and service category
- Business owner
- Data categories accessed
- Access level
- Risk level
- Contract / DPA status
- Security review date
- Subprocessor considerations
- Authentication / credential model
- Data retention and deletion commitments
- Incident notification obligations
- Geographic processing considerations where relevant
- Exit / offboarding plan
- Next review date

## Approval principle

A vendor is not approved merely because it is popular, inexpensive, or easy to integrate. Approval must be proportionate to the sensitivity and operational importance of the data or system involved.

## Access levels

- None
- Limited
- Sensitive
- Critical

Vendors receiving verification evidence, identity information, consumer-report information, or administrative credentials require heightened review.

## Lifecycle

Inventory → Review required → In review → Approved / Approved with conditions / Rejected → Periodic review → Offboarded

## Offboarding

Remove credentials, terminate integrations, verify deletion/return obligations where applicable, and document any data or records that must remain after termination.
