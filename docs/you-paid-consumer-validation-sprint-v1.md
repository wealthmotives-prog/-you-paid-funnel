# You Paid — Consumer Validation Sprint v1
**Date:** October 24, 2026 · **Status:** proposed, not yet executed

## Hypothesis
People who have fragmented or long employment histories may value a secure, permission-based place to organize their own work history, distinguish self-declared information from employer-confirmed information, and request confirmation *once the verification service is operational*. This is not a credit bureau furnishing or credit-score product today.

## Three target segments to sample (do not pre-select winners)
1. Shift and hourly employees with multiple recent jobs.
2. Independent contractors and gig workers whose tenure and schedules are difficult to summarize.
3. Workers who frequently need proof of prior employment for legitimate applications.

Aim for 5–10 conversations in each segment, prioritizing a diversity of circumstances. Recruiting convenience samples creates selection bias. Do not ask participants to provide SSNs, paystubs, IDs, employer contact details or logins during exploratory interviews.

## Disclosure shown before all prototypes or price questions
**Current:** Self-declared work-history organization; member control and prelaunch account tools. **Not currently live:** employer confirmation intake, evidence upload, credit bureau reporting or a credit account. Work records are unverified unless and until independently supported and reviewed. No credit score impact is promised.

## Interview script (15–20 minutes)
- When did you last need to recall or prove where and when you worked? What happened?
- What records did you use, what was hardest, and how long did it take?
- How often does this happen in a year? What was the practical cost or consequence?
- What tools, employer HR portals, tax records, or existing services do you already use?
- Show the current, clearly labeled prototype. Ask the user to describe **in their own words** what You Paid does *today* and what it **does not** do.
- Ask them to attempt a fictional history declaration in a controlled demo; note confusion and completion time. Do not collect real PII.
- Without introducing credit impacts, ask whether they would use this service again and in what situation.
- Ask what they would need to trust a future employer-verification request; ask employer-side participants about response burden separately.
- End by asking about alternatives and willingness to pay; do not lead with a suggested amount.

## Experiment setup
- 20–30 interviews; at least 10 supervised prototype demonstrations.
- Randomize clearly disclosed price concepts across separate respondents: free core organizer + paid verified request (future only), one-time paid organizer/export, recurring membership for ongoing organization. These are **research concepts**, not offers or live features.
- Record respondent segment, qualifying task, current alternative, clarity of expectations, demonstration completion, willingness to pay, and objections. No recordings or personal details unless an appropriate consent and retention plan is established.
- Ask what price they would realistically pay; distinguish statements from actual purchases.

## Suggested success thresholds (to approve **further research**, not public launch)
- At least 80% of demo participants can explain the current service without expecting active bureau furnishing, score gains or live employer confirmations.
- At least 60% of interviewed target users report a concrete, recurring or consequential work-history problem.
- At least 30% can name a realistic paid use case independent of improving a credit score.
- No observed misrepresentation or unauthorized sensitive data collection; any occurrence triggers immediate pause and correction.
Thresholds are founder-defined hypotheses and may be changed **before** recruitment, not retrospectively to declare success.

## Research-to-product decision
1. If understanding is low, improve explanations before adding acquisition spend.
2. If the problem is real but recurring willingness is low, test transaction pricing rather than assuming a subscription.
3. If employer confirmation demand dominates, schedule independent employer-operations research; do not enable real intake without P0 safeguards.
4. Expand only after evidence of demand and appropriate privacy/compliance review.

## Product/legal guardrails
No claims of automatic employment verification, bureau furnishing, guaranteed credit score benefit, predated account opening, or actual historic attendance from estimates. Keep current member portal declaration labels self-declared/unverified; never alter user-submitted factual dates to generate credit history. Refund, data deletion, member disputes, security monitoring and appropriate terms must be reconciled before paid scaling.

## Engineering adjustment this sprint
Member app metadata changed from “Your verified work history” to a truthful description distinguishing self-declared and independently verified information. Ledger label changed to “Work History Ledger.” These are copy-only changes; no intake or database status was enabled.
