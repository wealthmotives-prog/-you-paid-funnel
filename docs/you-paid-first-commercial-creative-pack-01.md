## Social copy (draft)

**Body:** "Meet You Paid—an emerging platform being built to organize work history with accuracy, transparency and future verification in mind. We're pursuing responsible pathways toward credit furnishing; we are not reporting accounts to credit bureaus today."
**CTA:** "Explore the project and get development updates at joinyoupaid.com."
**Legal/accuracy check:** do not infer the site has functioning email opt-in without end-to-end QA.

## Creative rules
- Actors or fictional users only; clearly label mock account previews and avoid fabricated testimonials.
- Avoid actual score dashboards, bureau logos, approvals, lender offers or tradeline screenshots.
- Do not suggest consumers earn payment history through hours worked; separate real credit obligation from employment proof.
- Avoid dated guaranteed release and avoid claiming verified credit line deliveries.
- Large accessible captions; contrast checked on mobile. Keep disclosures visible long enough to read and place core qualifications in spoken wording where material.
- Final copy subject to product status and advertising compliance review.
