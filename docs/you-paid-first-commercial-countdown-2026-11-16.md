# You Paid — First Commercial Countdown & Launch Gates
Created October 24, 2026. Proposed first prelaunch awareness commercial: **Monday, November 16, 2026**. NOT a date for live furnishing or employer intake.

## Campaign definition
Purpose: explain the mission, build qualified awareness and guide people to a truthful prelaunch information/waitlist experience at https://joinyoupaid.com.
Do not imply approved CRA furnishing, currently reportable tradelines, loan issuance, guaranteed improvement, verified employer records, or delivered services still under development.
Primary CTA: "Explore You Paid and join the updates list." Paid founder checkout is a distinct offering requiring current scope, clear terms, refund/fulfillment reconciliation and review before promoted advertising.
Proposed distribution: 15-second vertical social video first; 30-second cut second. These are hypotheses, not yet published media.

## Gate checklist
- [ ] Written commercial copy and subtitles match prelaunch product (no live furnishing).
- [ ] Landing page captures honest consent to marketing where applicable.
- [ ] Privacy links, data requests and deletion contact routes are functional; no made-up rights/SLAs.
- [ ] No pixels/optional tracking enabled before legally necessary notices/preferences.
- [ ] Test signup flow, mobile form, confirmations and deduplication.
- [ ] No promise of credit outcome, no paid testimonial from untested service.
- [ ] Person responsible for customer replies and refunds (if checkout linked) confirmed.
- [ ] Budget cap, platform account, distribution and success metrics approved by founder.
- [ ] Screenshot and archive final ad + claims substantiation + destination page.
- [ ] Final written launch authorization.

## Proposed measures (targets, NOT achievements)
- Top-of-funnel: 100–300 qualified visits during first test week; source and spend recorded.
- Lead conversion: target 5% consented signup rate as an initial directional hypothesis; not benchmark-backed.
- Message comprehension: 80%+ of 10 test viewers correctly say furnishing is NOT live.
- Quality: zero known misleading claims; unsubscribe/complaint response coverage.
- If paid, do not automatically scale based solely on inexpensive clicks. Compare consented lead cost and signal quality.

## Product/furnishing roadmap (separate from first commercial)
Furnisher applications, substantive contract/product design, Metro 2 implementation and bureau onboarding continue as separate critical work. Date of first awareness ad cannot reasonably be represented as first bureau acceptance or first reported tradeline.
