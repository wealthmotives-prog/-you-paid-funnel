# You Paid — Founding Network and Furnishing Research Charter
**October 24, 2026 — Working draft for founders / counsel. No furnishing or guaranteed scoring is live.**

## Nonnegotiable vision
Pursue a lawful, operationally sound relationship for furnishing qualifying account data to credit reporting agencies. Founding-member recruitment is community-building and evidence of interest, **not a petition that obligates any CRA to contract or a substitute for standing/cause of action**. Preserve factual business correspondence and outcomes for counsel to evaluate if a concrete unlawful refusal occurs.

## Two tracks
**Business credentialing (offline team):** verify company entity documents and DBA, EIN, operational office/contact channels, institutional bank relationships, product contracts, written underwriting/servicing rules where relevant, data retention, dispute and investigation processes, ownership/authority, cybersecurity program and vendor agreements. Record each bureau's published requirements and actual application responses.
**Digital/community (You Paid):** truthful waitlist and founding-member opt-in (distinct from purchase); publish clearly dated capability status; allow withdrawal/unsubscribe; store receipt timestamp, exact consent version, acquisition source, policy version and campaign; forbid implication of bureau approval or imminent tradeline. Outsourced ad agency uses approved copy and cannot introduce unapproved promises.

## Important accounting separation
1. Work performed / attendance = employment or activity evidence, not by itself a debt payment.
2. WorkScore or performance grade = internal analytics describing reliable evidence, leave and adjustments. Do not call it a credit score.
3. Actual credit account = independently documented extension of credit, agreed obligation, account opening, liability, terms, actual transactions, amount due, due date, consideration, payment/application, disputes and reversals.
4. On-time payment status = result of truthful servicing records and applicable reporting definitions; cannot be inferred solely from a high work grade.
5. Reported credit tradeline = only after qualifying agreement, data accuracy controls, approved reporting channel and correct Metro 2 treatment. No synthetic or backdated accounts.

## Research question / potential innovation
May verified labor or deliverables lawfully discharge or satisfy a **genuine contractual monetary obligation** through agreed noncash performance? If considered, external credit/counsel review must determine lawful contract substance, fair valuation, consumer protection, actual creditor acceptance, periodic due dates, tax/wage issues and whether any agency would accept the classification. Performance cannot be *renamed* a payment by formula absent a legitimate obligation and satisfaction event.

## Prospective evidence-backed model
- Genuine servicing ledger: account agreement; date opened; advance or consideration; obligation events; due dates; payments or authorized noncash satisfaction accepted; current balance; delinquency/corrections; disputes.
- Separate work ledger: historical claim; period; self-declared vs independently verified source; attendance/proof; reviewer; disputed/corrected; outcome.
- Separate scoring ledger: transparent internal score calculation, version, data sources, confidence, sample-size/leave handling; never export as Metro 2 account status.
- Controlled mapping: only map actual account events to appropriate Metro 2 fields after CDIA access and bureau test approval.
- Mandatory guardrails: no cherry-picking only positive months or suppressing accurate delinquencies; periodic accuracy policies and reasonable dispute investigations.

## Founding network campaign
Original headline concept: "You showed up. We're building a way for that history to count."
Core: "Join the founding community shaping You Paid. We are developing transparent work records and pursuing lawful credit-reporting pathways. Credit bureau furnishing is not currently active, and no score or approval result can be promised."
CTA: "Follow our progress" / "Join the founding updates list." Do not describe a waitlist as a pending bureau application or class-action petition.
Optional separate advocacy opt-in only after reviewed scope, purpose, privacy, sharing and legal basis. Don't bundle with product enrollment or imply it guarantees litigation.

## Outsourced agency brief
Deliverables: one 15s + one 30s commercial; 9:16 captioned masters and 1:1 cutdowns; 3 concept routes; ad platform accounts and spend remain under company control; broad advertising and derivative use rights; editable project deliverables; no third-party assets without rights.
Approval gates: founder + claims owner approve all scripts, onscreen labels, disclaimer readability, subtitles and landing-page destinations. Contract should cover IP assignment/license, confidentiality, disclosures, subcontractors, data-processing limits, portfolio use and ad access ownership. Separate creative scope from campaign management fee.

## Research acceptance criteria
- Can member accurately explain services live today?
- Do firm corporate requirements and account design support a genuine credit relationship?
- Is CDIA access available and a Metro 2 category appropriate to the actual account?
- Has a bureau or accepted data processor reviewed test submissions?
- Is the furnishing policy compliant with FCRA/Regulation V and able to handle unfavorable facts and disputes?
- Does marketing evidence show real demand without suggesting legal rights or guaranteed reporting?
