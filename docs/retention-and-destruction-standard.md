# You Paid Retention & Destruction Standard v0.1

Status: Draft operating standard

## Principle

You Paid should not retain every record forever merely because storage is inexpensive. Retention must be tied to a defined purpose, verification need, dispute requirement, security need, accounting obligation, reporting obligation, legal hold, or other documented basis.

## Each data category must define

- Examples of records in the category
- Business purpose
- Retention rule
- Trigger event
- Destruction or de-identification method
- Legal-hold override
- Owner
- Approval status
- Review date

## Current categories

- Waitlist / marketing
- Member account
- Employment records
- Verification evidence
- Disputes and corrections
- Reporting transmissions
- Founder/payment records
- Security logs
- Privacy-rights requests

## Rules

1. Draft retention periods are not operational deletion rules until approved.
2. Legal holds override ordinary destruction where applicable.
3. Deletion requests require eligibility review; some limited records may need to remain for audit, fraud prevention, dispute history, or legal obligations.
4. When a record is deleted from a primary system, downstream copies and authorized destinations must be considered where applicable.
5. Automated deletion jobs should be auditable.
6. Destruction must be appropriate to the medium and sensitivity of the data.

## Before verification scales

Approve category-specific retention periods with legal/security input and implement automated expiry or review queues where feasible.
