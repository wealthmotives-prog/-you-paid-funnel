The You Paid Data Network

From fragmented work records to permissioned, verifiable infrastructure.

The long-term opportunity is larger than a single consumer feature. You Paid is being designed as a network that can receive work evidence from authoritative sources, normalize it, preserve history, resolve disputes, and make verified data portable when a lawful downstream use exists.

Consumer layer

Identity, consent, employment relationships, member-visible history, corrections, and sharing choices.

Provider layer

Employers, payroll, timekeeping, gig, staffing, bank-derived payroll evidence, and other qualifying sources.

Verification layer

Evidence strength, event dates, verification dates, source provenance, monthly normalization, confidence, and audit history.

Dispute layer

Consumer challenges, source review, modification, deletion, and correction records.

Transmission layer

Destination, format, period, transmission status, acknowledgements, rejections, and corrections.

Utility layer

Potential member-authorized use by reporting companies, lenders, landlords, fintechs, or other qualified users once real relationships exist.

Why the dataset can become valuable

Consistency is longitudinal.

A single work event says little. A source-backed record across months and years can show tenure, recurring activity, continuity, multiple-income patterns, neutral interruptions, and correction history. The value grows as the record becomes deeper and more reliable.

The moat

A competitor can copy a landing page. It cannot instantly reproduce years of permissioned, source-backed, longitudinal work-history data with provenance and dispute history.
