Frequently asked questions

Understand the product before you join.

The questions below cover what You Paid is, what it is not, and what is still being built.

What is You Paid?

You Paid is building a verified work-history and consistency platform. The core idea is to preserve evidence of employment tenure, showing up, and qualifying work activity over time.

Is You Paid a credit-repair company?

No. The current product direction is verification and maintenance of work-history data. You Paid is not currently marketed as a credit-repair service.

Does You Paid currently report to credit bureaus?

No guaranteed furnishing relationship is represented as live today. Consumer-reporting pathways remain under development and must be resolved before any reporting claim is made.

Will You Paid increase my credit score?

No score outcome is guaranteed. Scoring models, data acceptance, and lender decisions are controlled by third parties.

Can my employment tenure predate the date I join You Paid?

Yes, if the historical employment fact can be supported. That does not backdate your You Paid relationship. Employment tenure and You Paid history are tracked separately.

What counts as verification?

Verification depends on evidence. Potential sources include employer confirmation, payroll information, recognized timekeeping information, supported documents, bank payroll evidence, or manual review. See the public Verification Standard for the intended framework.

Can I verify myself just by saying I worked?

Self-attestation may help start a record or review, but it should not independently create a verified event without sufficient supporting evidence.

What happens if I lose my job or go on leave?

You Paid is being designed so that job loss, protected leave, a source outage, or another neutral interruption does not automatically become negative performance.

What is Founding Access?

Founding Access is a separate one-time prelaunch program with $1, $10, and $100 levels. It provides founder recognition and the stated founder privileges. It is separate from any future recurring verification subscription.

Is Founding Access an investment or equity?

No. It is not stock, equity, a security, a loan, a deposit, or a right to profits or ownership.

Why is there no founder cap?

The scarcity is time-based rather than quantity-based. Founding status is intended to remain open during the prelaunch era and close when You Paid enters public launch.

What data will You Paid collect?

The intended approach is to collect data needed to establish identity, work relationships, verification events, account operation, payments, and support. The platform should avoid collecting unrelated data merely because it is available.

Can I correct inaccurate data?

The product is being designed with a visible correction and dispute path. Accuracy is a core requirement of the record, not an optional feature.

Who operates You Paid?

You Paid is operated by 850 Lifestyle LLC and is intended to be offered under the You Paid trade name.
