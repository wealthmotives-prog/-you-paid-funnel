Frequently asked questions

Understand the record before you trust the outcome.

You Paid is being built around historical and ongoing work verification. These answers distinguish what we can structure and verify from what still depends on external reporting relationships.

What is You Paid?

A prelaunch verified work-data platform being built to turn supported historical and ongoing employment activity into a longitudinal, portable record.

Can You Paid verify work I performed before I joined?

Yes, conceptually and technically, if reliable evidence supports the historical facts. The record should preserve the actual work-event date and the later You Paid verification date. Your You Paid membership date is not backdated.

Is historical verification the same as backdating?

No. Backdating would imply the You Paid relationship existed earlier than it did. Historical verification instead records a real earlier event and separately records when You Paid verified that event.

What sources can support historical activity?

Potential sources include employer records, payroll providers, timekeeping systems, staffing and gig platforms, bank-derived payroll evidence, tax/payroll documents, pay statements, dated work records, and other qualifying evidence.

Will self-attestation alone create verified history?

No. Member statements can start an intake or dispute, but verified status should require sufficient supporting evidence.

Does You Paid currently report to Experian, Equifax, or TransUnion?

No live nationwide bureau furnishing relationship is currently represented. The system is being built to become reporting-ready while specific destination relationships, accepted data formats, onboarding requirements, and compliance operations are pursued.

Could You Paid eventually report historical information?

That is one of the reporting paths being pursued. Whether a specific destination accepts a particular historical work-data field depends on that destination's accepted data model, contracts, operational requirements, and applicable law.

Is Metro 2 definitely the format You Paid will use?

No final claim is being made yet. Traditional consumer data furnishing often uses Metro 2, but You Paid will not force work-performance data into an inappropriate format merely to resemble a conventional credit account. The accepted destination and ingestion channel must be established first.

Could You Paid become a specialty consumer reporting company?

That is a strategic path under evaluation. Employment information already exists within the broader consumer-reporting ecosystem, but becoming or operating through a consumer reporting framework would create substantial FCRA, access, disclosure, dispute, security, and permissible-purpose obligations.

What is the employer/data-provider model?

You Paid intends to develop relationships with employers, payroll, timekeeping, staffing, gig, and other qualifying sources so authoritative data can be contributed through defined authorization and correction processes.

What happens if my work data is wrong?

The backend now has a dedicated dispute structure. The member-facing process is being built to support receipt, investigation, source contact, modification, deletion, and documented resolution.

What happens if I lose my job or go on leave?

You Paid is designed to distinguish neutral interruptions from actual unsupported or failed performance. Job loss, approved leave, or a data-source outage should not automatically become derogatory.

Does paying more make my work record stronger?

No. Payment affects services or founder privileges. Evidence determines verification.

What is Founding Access?

A separate one-time prelaunch program with $1, $10, and $100 levels. It is not equity, an investment, a credit account, or a purchase of guaranteed bureau reporting.

Will You Paid improve my credit score?

No score outcome is guaranteed. Future impact depends on what data is accepted, where it is used, and how independent scoring or underwriting systems treat it.

Who operates You Paid?

850 Lifestyle LLC operates the You Paid platform and is intended to offer the service under the You Paid trade name.
