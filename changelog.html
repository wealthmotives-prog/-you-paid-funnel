Public changelog

Show the work.

This changelog records material product and infrastructure decisions so public claims can evolve with the actual system.

October 10, 2026

Historical work-data breakthrough

Reframed You Paid around historical and ongoing work verification rather than post-enrollment activity alone. Added the two-date rule: underlying event date and You Paid verification date remain separate.

October 10, 2026

Reporting-readiness architecture

Expanded the backend with evidence levels, source entities, historical-event flags, verification timestamps, data-provider records, provider authorizations, consumer dispute tracking, reporting destinations, and transmission-status records.

October 10, 2026

Data-network and employer strategy

Added dedicated How It Works, For Employers, and Data Network pages to describe provider contributions, historical verification, reporting pathways, and the long-term work-data infrastructure opportunity.

October 10, 2026

Trust center expansion

Added Who We Are, What We're Building, Trust & Transparency, Quokka Mission, Verification Standard, FAQ, Product Status, Security & Data Practices, and public product documentation.

October 9, 2026

Founding Access system

Built the $1, $10, and $100 Founding Access architecture, including tier selection, terms acceptance, entitlement tracking, upgrade math, and refund-aware membership logic.

October 2026

Verified Work Ledger MVP

Created member authentication and the initial work-ledger model for employment records, verification events, monthly work cycles, and audit history.

September–October 2026

Production infrastructure

Connected the public site to GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, joinyoupaid.com, branded email, waitlist capture, and first-party funnel analytics.

Change-control principle

If a material product capability or public claim changes, the relevant status, disclosure, FAQ, verification standard, or security page should change with it.
