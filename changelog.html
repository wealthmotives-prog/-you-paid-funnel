Show the work.

A prelaunch company earns trust by documenting progress. This changelog records meaningful public-facing product and infrastructure milestones, not every internal code change.

October 10, 2026

Trust center expansion

Added Who We Are, What We're Building, Trust & Transparency, Quokka Mission, Verification Standard v1.0, FAQ, Product Status, Security & Data Practices, and this public changelog. Refocused the homepage on the verified-work product rather than the quokka mission.

October 9, 2026

Founding Access system

Built the $1, $10, and $100 Founding Access architecture, including tier selection, terms acceptance, entitlement tracking, upgrade math, refund-aware membership logic, and operator visibility.

October 2026

Operator portal

Added authorized operator access for member, founder, waitlist, verification, and product-control views.

October 2026

Verified Work Ledger MVP

Created member authentication and the initial work-ledger model for employment records, verification events, monthly work cycles, and audit history.

September–October 2026

Production infrastructure

Connected the public site to GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, joinyoupaid.com, branded email, waitlist capture, and first-party funnel analytics.

Change-control principle

If a material public claim changes, the relevant status, disclosure, FAQ, or verification standard should change with it. The goal is for public documentation to track the actual product rather than lag behind it.
