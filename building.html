What we're building

A verified work record that gets stronger with time.

The core product is a member-controlled history of employment tenure, work presence, continuity, and qualifying verified activity. The goal is to preserve evidence in a way that is clear, auditable, and increasingly useful.

Core architecture

Four layers, kept deliberately separate.

1. Employment record

Who the member worked for, the actual dates of the relationship, and whether the relationship is active, ended, on leave, or otherwise unresolved.

2. Verification events

Evidence tied to specific periods or events, such as payroll, timekeeping, employer confirmation, bank payroll evidence, member documents, or manual review.

3. Monthly work cycles

Normalized monthly records showing verified workdays, clock-ins, continuity, or other supported measures.

4. Audit history

A record of what changed, when it changed, and why, so verification is not a black box.

Important distinction

Employment tenure is not You Paid account age.

If someone has worked for an employer since 2022 and joins You Paid in 2026, the employment commencement may reflect 2022 if it can be supported. Their You Paid history still begins in 2026. We do not need to blur those facts to make the record useful.

Example

Verified employment tenure: 4 years 7 months

You Paid history: 8 months

Verified cycles: 8

Verification standard

Not every source should be treated as equally strong.

Source provenance

Each verified event should identify where the evidence came from and the period it supports.

Confidence and review

Automated verification can handle clear evidence; ambiguous evidence should move to human review instead of being forced into a positive or negative state.

Neutral states

Leave, job loss, missing data, or a temporary verification gap should not automatically be treated as failure.

Correction path

Members need a visible way to challenge or correct inaccurate verification data.
