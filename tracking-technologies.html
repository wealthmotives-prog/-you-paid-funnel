Transparency

Tracking Technologies Notice

Effective October 11, 2026

What we use today

The current public site uses browser storage and first-party session identifiers to support site functionality, waitlist attribution, and basic product analytics. The site may record events such as page views, waitlist-form starts, selected calls to action, scroll milestones, campaign parameters, referring page, and landing page.

Essential vs. optional technologies

Some browser storage is used for basic product operation, such as maintaining a session identifier or retaining limited local state. Nonessential advertising, retargeting, or third-party behavioral tracking should not be activated silently. If those technologies are introduced, You Paid will update this notice and add appropriate preference controls where required.

Advertising pixels

The current public configuration does not represent Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, or similar advertising pixels as active. Their presence should not be assumed from placeholder code, future plans, or campaign links.

Do Not Track and privacy signals

Browser and jurisdictional privacy signals continue to evolve. You Paid will evaluate legally required opt-out and preference mechanisms before activating advertising or cross-context behavioral tracking that requires them.

Data minimization

Analytics should be limited to information reasonably useful for operating, securing, and improving the service. We do not need unrelated behavioral data merely because a vendor can collect it.
