Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

Who operates You Paid?

You Paid is operated by 850 Lifestyle LLC and is intended to be offered under the You Paid trade name. The product is currently in prelaunch development.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use You Paid, we may collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, work situation, employment information, role or title, employment dates, founder-tier selections, support messages, consent records, and other information you choose to submit.

As the member ledger develops, we may also process verification-related information such as work-presence events, payroll or timekeeping evidence, employer or source information, verification status, monthly work-cycle data, correction or dispute information, and audit records. A future production integration may require additional disclosures before new categories of sensitive information are collected.

We may collect limited technical and attribution information such as session identifiers, campaign and UTM parameters, landing-page URL, referring page, browser interaction events, IP-derived security signals, device/browser information made available by infrastructure providers, and the date and time of submissions or consent.

How we use information

We use information to operate accounts and the waitlist, maintain member records, support verification workflows, communicate about product development, provide founder/member experiences, prevent abuse, measure product performance, respond to support or privacy requests, maintain security and audit records, and comply with applicable obligations.

Verification data

A central purpose of You Paid is to organize supported work-history information. Payment or founder status does not determine whether a work fact is verified. Verification is intended to depend on evidence and the applicable verification standard.

Service providers

Current infrastructure includes Vercel for web hosting, Supabase for authentication/backend data processing and storage, GitHub for source-code hosting and deployment workflows, and Google Workspace for company email. Additional providers, including a payment processor or verification-data providers, may be added only as those functions are activated.

Payments

Live secure payment processing is not represented as active until the payment rail is actually connected and tested. When payments become active, payment-card or bank-account credentials should be handled by the authorized payment processor rather than stored directly in ordinary You Paid application tables except where a lawful and necessary record is required.

Credit reporting and downstream use

You Paid does not currently promise that member information is furnished to Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, or another consumer reporting agency. We do not guarantee a credit-score, approval, or underwriting result. Any future external data-sharing or reporting service will require appropriate terms, disclosures, permissions, and compliance processes before launch.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information merely because a person joins the waitlist or creates a member account. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate the product, when a member directs or authorizes a permitted sharing workflow, when required by law, to protect rights or security, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, verification history, product development, consent records, support, security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and other legitimate operational purposes. As the service matures, retention periods should be refined by data category rather than applying one indefinite retention rule to everything.

Security

We use administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the current stage of the product, including authenticated account access and backend row-level access controls. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure. Our current security design principles are described on the Security & Data Practices page.

Your choices and corrections

You may request correction or deletion of eligible account or waitlist information, withdraw marketing consent, or raise a privacy concern by emailing privacy@joinyoupaid.com. Verification-specific corrections or disputes may require preservation of limited audit information so the record can show that a change occurred and why.

Communications

If you consent to product-development or early-access communications, you may opt out of promotional email communications using the unsubscribe method provided in those messages.

Children

The current prelaunch service is intended for U.S. adults age 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
