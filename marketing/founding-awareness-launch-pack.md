# You Paid — Founding Awareness Launch Pack (draft only)
Status: NOT PUBLISHED. No budget approved.

## Campaign A: "Your work has a story"
Audience: employees with long work histories.
Hook: "You've shown up. You've put in the hours. Your work history tells a story worth documenting."
Body: "You Paid is building member-controlled tools to organize employment history and develop evidence-backed work verification. Join the founding community and help shape what's next."
CTA: "Explore You Paid" -> https://joinyoupaid.com
Guard: no bureau claims, no credit score benefits, no employer-contact promises.

## Campaign B: "Recognition without punishment"
Audience: people skeptical of conventional credit-builder experiences.
Hook: "We believe work consistency deserves a fairer way to be recognized."
Body: "Our developing WorkScore concept separates documented work patterns from negative credit reporting. We're building carefully, starting with records you can understand and correct."
CTA: "See what we're building" -> https://joinyoupaid.com
Guard: clear future-looking language; no claim of reporting activity.

## Campaign C: "Founding builders"
Audience: early supporters who want product input.
Hook: "Don't just watch the next chapter of financial technology. Help shape it."
Body: "You Paid is building a verification-first work-history platform. Explore the founding community and the product roadmap. Availability and founding terms apply."
CTA: "Discover founding access" -> https://joinyoupaid.com
Guard: confirm current tier prices, benefits, refund terms and payment mode on site before publication.
