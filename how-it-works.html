How it works

Past work. Current consistency. One verified record.

You Paid is being built to verify real work activity whether it happened before or after a member joins. The critical distinction is simple: the date an event happened is not the same thing as the date You Paid verified it.

1

Connect

Establish the member's employment, payroll, timekeeping, gig, staffing, or other qualifying work source.

2

Verify

Confirm historical and ongoing activity using supported evidence. Older events can be verified later without pretending You Paid observed them at the time.

3

Build the record

Normalize verified events into a longitudinal work-history record with source, period, status, and audit history.

The two-date rule

What happened then. What we verified now.

A historical work event can be legitimate even when verification occurs years later. You Paid is being designed to preserve both dates so the record remains transparent.

Illustrative record

Underlying event: July 14, 2024

Source: employer timekeeping record

Verified by You Paid: October 10, 2026

Status: verified

Historical verification

Joining today should not erase evidence of yesterday.

If reliable evidence supports that a member worked for an employer before joining You Paid, the historical employment and work events may be recorded with their actual dates. The You Paid membership date remains the date the member actually joined. Historical verification is not backdating a You Paid account.
