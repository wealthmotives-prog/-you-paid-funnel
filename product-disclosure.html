Prelaunch Product Disclosure

Effective October 10, 2026

What You Paid is today

You Paid is a prelaunch verified work-history and consistency platform operated by 850 Lifestyle LLC and intended to be offered under the You Paid trade name. Current public materials describe a product under development rather than a completed consumer-reporting or lending service.

Verified Work Ledger

The core product direction is a record that separates employment tenure, You Paid membership history, verification events, monthly work cycles, and audit history. A member submission does not automatically become verified. Verification is intended to depend on evidence and the applicable review process.

No current credit furnishing promise

You Paid does not currently represent that it is furnishing member information to Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, or another consumer reporting agency. No tradeline, bureau reporting, score increase, or underwriting result is guaranteed.

Employment tenure and You Paid history are different

If supported evidence shows that a member began an employment relationship before joining You Paid, the employment start date may reflect that historical fact. The member's You Paid relationship and You Paid verification history still begin when those relationships actually begin. You Paid does not intend to fabricate account age or historical performance.

Verification states

Current design distinguishes states such as pending, verified, neutral, disputed, unavailable, or rejected. The purpose is to avoid treating uncertainty or a neutral interruption as if it were proven positive or negative performance.

Founding Access is separate

The $1, $10, and $100 Founding Access levels are separate one-time prelaunch program purchases governed by the Founding Access Terms. Founder level does not make work-history information more accurate or more likely to be marked verified.

Future subscription

A future recurring membership may support ongoing work-data verification, record maintenance, enhanced member tools, or other services. Final pricing, included features, billing terms, and cancellation rules are not represented as final until the subscription is actually launched.

No borrowing required by the current prelaunch experience

Joining the waitlist, creating a member account, or viewing the current work ledger does not require borrowing money, paying interest, or carrying a credit balance.

External use remains under development

The long-term product direction may include member-authorized sharing or other external utility. Specific consumer-report furnishing pathways, Metro 2 compatibility, third-party underwriting acceptance, and scoring treatment remain unresolved unless later announced with appropriate supporting terms and disclosures.

Quokka mission is separate

The Quokka Mission is a separate long-term brand and conservation initiative. It does not influence work-verification decisions and is described separately at our Quokka Mission page.

Where to verify current claims

See the Product Status, Verification Standard v1.0, and Trust & Transparency pages for the current public description of what is built, building, or unresolved.
