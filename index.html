The core idea

Turn showing up into a record that can compound over time.

Work history already contains signals of consistency, continuity, and responsibility. You Paid is being built to collect qualifying evidence, verify it, organize it into a clear longitudinal record, and give members a better way to understand and use that history.

1

Connect the history

Establish employment or work relationships and the period they actually existed.

2

Verify activity

Use supported evidence to confirm work presence, continuity, tenure, or other clearly defined activity.

3

Build the record

Preserve verified history month by month without pretending the You Paid relationship existed before it actually did.

A simple distinction
Employment tenure can be historical. Your You Paid history starts when you actually join.

Truth first. Utility second.

We do not need to manufacture account age or invent financial history to make work consistency meaningful. Our aim is to preserve real facts, identify the source behind them, and build utility on top of data that can withstand scrutiny.

Why newcomers should feel comfortable

Clear about what exists. Clear about what does not.

Confidence should come from verifiable behavior, not hype. These are the standards we are building around now.

Real operator identity

You Paid is operated by 850 Lifestyle LLC and uses branded company email and published legal/privacy pages.

Evidence determines verification

Payments, founder status, or membership level do not make a work record more true. Verification should be based on evidence.

No hidden backdating

Historical employment tenure and You Paid membership history are tracked separately.

No guaranteed downstream outcome

We do not currently promise bureau furnishing, score increases, approval, or a particular underwriting result.

Member-visible status

Our member portal is being designed to distinguish pending, verified, and other record states rather than hiding uncertainty.

Human accountability

Members have clear channels for support, privacy, and legal questions as the platform develops.
