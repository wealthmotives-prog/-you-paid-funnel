The problem → the solution

You can work for years and still have most of that consistency invisible when creditworthiness is judged.

Traditional credit files focus heavily on borrowing and repayment. We believe verified work history deserves a clearer place in the picture too.

What happens today

  • You show up month after month.
  • You build years of employment history.
  • Your pay can change without your consistency disappearing.
  • Much of that history sits scattered across employers and payroll systems.

What You Paid is building

  • Verify real historical and current work activity.
  • Preserve that history in one member record.
  • Show tenure and consistency without requiring a specific income level.
  • Pursue responsible ways for that verified history to become useful in creditworthiness and financial decisions.

Fast answers

The questions people actually ask.

Does You Paid report to Equifax, Experian, or TransUnion today?

Not yet. We are building the verification and reporting-readiness infrastructure now and pursuing appropriate external reporting pathways.

Can old work history count?

Yes, if reliable evidence supports it. A historical work event can be verified later while keeping its original date.

Do I need to make a certain amount of money?

You Paid is being designed around verified work history and consistency, not a minimum income threshold for recognition of that history.

Will this raise my credit score?

No specific score result can be promised. Future impact depends on where verified You Paid data is accepted and how independent scoring or underwriting systems choose to use it.

Is this credit repair?

No. The core product is verified work-history infrastructure, not deletion of accurate credit information.
