Our Quokka Mission

Build something valuable. Leave something good behind.

You Paid is being built around a simple belief: real value can exist before conventional systems fully recognize it. That belief extends beyond finance.

🌿

Growth with a conservation purpose.

As You Paid grows, we intend for our ability to support quokka conservation, responsible research, habitat protection, education, and qualified conservation partnerships to grow with it.

This is a long-term mission statement, not a representation that You Paid currently operates a zoological or conservation facility or has an existing animal-transfer arrangement.

01

Awareness

Use the You Paid platform to introduce more people to quokkas and the importance of responsible conservation.

02

Support

Develop measurable ways for growth in the You Paid ecosystem to support legitimate conservation efforts.

03

Partnership

Pursue relationships with appropriate conservation, research, and zoological organizations.

04
