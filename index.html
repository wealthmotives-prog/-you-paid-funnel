Why You Paid

Recognition before remediation.

People already show discipline every day. They work, juggle schedules, take care of families, hit deadlines, and keep going. You Paid is being built around the belief that this kind of consistency deserves to be seen.

1

Verify

Confirm legitimate employment or qualifying work activity through approved sources.

2

Satisfy

Qualifying verified performance satisfies the monthly PIK Performance Obligation under the account terms.

3

Build

Month by month, the consumer builds a growing record of satisfactory account performance.

The feeling
“I’ve been working for years. Why shouldn’t that consistency count for something?”

Recognition before remediation.

You Paid isn’t being built to make people feel like a problem that needs fixing. The idea starts with something simpler: recognize the value that’s already there, then build from it.

Our Quokka Mission

Build something valuable. Leave something good behind.

You Paid is being built around a simple belief: real value can exist before conventional systems fully recognize it. That belief extends beyond finance.

🌿

Growth with a conservation purpose.

As You Paid grows, we intend for our ability to support quokka conservation, responsible research, habitat protection, education, and qualified conservation partnerships to grow with it.

This is a long-term mission statement, not a representation that You Paid currently operates a zoological or conservation facility or has an existing animal-transfer arrangement.

01

Awareness

Use the You Paid platform to introduce more people to quokkas and the importance of responsible conservation.

02

Support

Develop measurable ways for growth in the You Paid ecosystem to support legitimate conservation efforts.

03

Partnership

Pursue relationships with appropriate conservation, research, and zoological organizations.

04

Long-term U.S. conservation ambition

Explore whether You Paid can help support a qualified U.S.-based conservation and educational program where people could responsibly observe and learn about quokkas.
