The breakthrough

The work date and the verification date do not have to be the same.

A real work event can happen years before a member discovers You Paid. If reliable evidence supports that event later, we can preserve both truths: when the work happened and when You Paid verified it.

That is historical verification. It is not pretending the You Paid relationship existed earlier than it did.
