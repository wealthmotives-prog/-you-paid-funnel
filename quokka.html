Our Quokka Mission

Build something valuable. Leave something good behind.

Our quokka mission is a separate long-term brand and impact initiative. It is not part of the financial verification logic of You Paid and it does not influence whose work history is verified.

🌿

Why quokkas?

The idea fits a broader You Paid belief: value can exist before conventional systems recognize it. We want the brand's growth to eventually support something positive beyond the product itself.

Long-term ambition

Growth with a conservation purpose.

As You Paid grows, we intend to explore responsible ways to support quokka conservation, habitat protection, research, education, and qualified conservation organizations.

Awareness

Use our reach to help more people learn about quokkas and responsible conservation.

Support

Develop measurable methods through which growth in the You Paid ecosystem may support legitimate conservation efforts.

Partnership

Pursue relationships only with appropriate conservation, research, educational, or zoological organizations.

Education

Explore future educational experiences that help people understand the species and the importance of habitat stewardship.

Important boundary

A mission, not a current conservation operation.

You Paid does not currently represent that it operates a zoological facility, runs a quokka breeding program, imports or transfers animals, or has an existing formal conservation partnership. Any future conservation activity would need to be lawful, qualified, transparent, and appropriately partnered.
