Accessibility

Accessibility Commitment

You Paid wants its website and member experience to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people who navigate by keyboard, use assistive technologies, need reduced motion, or require stronger visual contrast.

Current design practices

  • Native links, buttons, form controls, labels, and semantic headings are preferred where practical.
  • The cinematic homepage respects the browser's reduced-motion preference.
  • Meaningful interactive graphics should have text equivalents or accessible labels.
  • Color should not be the only way important status information is communicated.

Work still in progress

You Paid is prelaunch and has not yet completed a full independent accessibility certification or audit. Formal keyboard-navigation testing, focus-state review, contrast measurement, screen-reader review, and future image/SVG alt-text review remain part of the readiness program.

Report a barrier

If something prevents you from using the site or member portal, email support@joinyoupaid.com and describe the page, device/browser, and barrier you encountered.
