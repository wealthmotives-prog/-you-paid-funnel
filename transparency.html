Trust & transparency

Confidence should come from what we can show you.

You Paid is prelaunch. We would rather be precise about what exists than make a bigger promise than the product can support.

Current status

What is real today.

Live website and member portal

Members can create accounts and begin maintaining employment information in the current prototype.

Work-ledger infrastructure

The backend separates employment records, verification events, monthly cycles, and audit history.

Operator controls

Authorized operators can review defined records and see founder/member activity.

Founding Access framework

$1, $10, and $100 founder tiers are structured as separate one-time access levels. Secure payment processing is not represented as live until it is actually activated.

What we do not currently promise

These are not marketing footnotes.

No guaranteed bureau reporting

You Paid does not currently promise that member information will be furnished to Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, or any other consumer reporting agency.

No score guarantee

We do not promise a credit-score increase or any particular scoring-model treatment.

No guaranteed approval

We do not promise lending, housing, insurance, employment, or underwriting approval.

No synthetic history

We do not intend to create false account age, fake limits, fabricated payments, or unsupported work history.

Data principles

The record should be more defensible than the marketing.

Minimum necessary data

Collect what is needed to provide and verify the service rather than gathering unrelated information simply because it is available.

Source awareness

Store the provenance of evidence whenever practical so a verified fact can be traced back to how it was established.

Corrections matter

Disputed or uncertain information should have a correction path and should not silently remain treated as verified.

Payments do not buy truth

Founder level, subscription price, or membership status should never change the evidentiary standard used to verify a person's work record.
