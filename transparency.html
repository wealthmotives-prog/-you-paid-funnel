Trust & transparency

Confidence should come from what we can show you.

You Paid is prelaunch. We are deliberately building the system to verify real historical and ongoing work data while keeping a hard line between what we can prove internally and what an external reporting destination has actually agreed to accept.

Current status

What is real today.

Live website and member portal

Members can create accounts and begin maintaining employment information in the current prototype.

Historical + ongoing ledger infrastructure

The backend now separates employment records, underlying event dates, You Paid verification dates, source entities, evidence levels, monthly cycles, and audit history.

Provider, dispute, and transmission architecture

The backend now includes structures for data providers, member/provider authorizations, consumer disputes, reporting destinations, and transmission status.

Founding Access framework

$1, $10, and $100 founder tiers are structured as separate one-time access levels. Secure payment processing is not represented as live until it is actually activated.

What we do not currently promise

These are not marketing footnotes.

No guaranteed bureau reporting

You Paid does not currently promise that member information will be furnished to Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, or any other consumer reporting agency.

No score guarantee

We do not promise a credit-score increase or any particular scoring-model treatment.

No guaranteed approval

We do not promise lending, housing, insurance, employment, or underwriting approval.

No synthetic history

We do not intend to create false You Paid account age, fake limits, fabricated payment history, or unsupported work events. Real historical work may be verified later when the evidence supports it.

Data principles

The record should be more defensible than the marketing.

Minimum necessary data

Collect what is needed to provide and verify the service rather than gathering unrelated information simply because it is available.

Source + date provenance

Preserve where the evidence came from, when the underlying activity occurred, and when You Paid actually verified it.

Corrections matter

Disputed or uncertain information should have a correction path and should not silently remain treated as verified.

Inspect the system

Trust should be auditable.

Historical verification is allowed by our model

A member can join today and present reliable evidence of work performed before enrollment. We record the underlying event date and the later verification date separately. That is different from backdating the You Paid relationship.

Reporting-ready is not reporting-live

We can structure records, disputes, providers, and transmissions now without claiming that Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, or any other destination currently accepts You Paid data.

Payments do not buy truth

Founder level, subscription price, or membership status should never change the evidentiary standard used to verify a person's work record.
